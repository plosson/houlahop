What your agent can use

Read, search and send. One command line for all of them.

GmailSlackWhatsAppGoogle ChatGoogle DriveJIRAConfluenceApple NotesDiscourseRSSRevolutKiteClaudeChatGPT…and more

Your passwords stay with you

You sign in once. agentio keeps the tokens encrypted.

Encrypted vaultTokens are encrypted on your machine (AES-256-GCM).
Your own hubAgents on other machines get access from a hub you run.
